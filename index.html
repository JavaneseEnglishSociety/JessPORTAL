About JESS

Peer teaching, at the scale of a movement.

JESS is a youth-led nonprofit providing free English education for students across Java. We train qualified middle and high school students to teach English themselves — building confidence, literacy, and community with every class.

Free for every student

Every class, every material, every session costs students nothing. It always will.

Speaking before grammar

Fluency grows through conversation, not memorisation. Students talk from day one.

Students become teachers

Our volunteers are teenagers who trained with us, then stayed to teach the next class.

Open across Java

We run fully online, so a student in any town joins the same class as everyone else.

    Volunteer

    Teach the class you wish you'd had.

    If you're a middle or high school student who's confident in English, you can train with us and lead your own class. No teaching experience needed — we'll get you there.

    Apply to volunteer
    1. Send us a messageUse the contact form below and tell us a little about yourself.
    2. Have a short chatAn informal conversation so we can hear how you speak and what you'd like to teach.
    3. Join teacher trainingA short orientation covering lesson structure, classroom confidence, and our materials.
    4. Take your first classYou'll co-teach with an experienced volunteer before leading on your own.
